Our IRS-certified volunteers prepare federal and Minnesota state returns, including all the credits you deserve.

🏛️

Federal Tax Return

Form 1040, W-2 income, self-employment (Schedule C), investment income, and more.

🌟

Earned Income Tax Credit (EITC)

Up to $7,830 federal credit for working families. Many eligible filers don't claim it — we make sure you do.

👶

Child Tax Credit

Up to $2,000 per qualifying child, including the Additional Child Tax Credit for refundable amounts.

🏠

MN Property Tax Refund (M1PR)

Minnesota renter and homeowner property tax refund — up to $2,440 for renters. Filed alongside your state return.

MN Form M1, Working Family Credit, and all applicable state deductions and credits.

🌍

ITIN Filer Support

We assist filers without a Social Security Number using an Individual Taxpayer Identification Number (ITIN).

Before You Arrive

What to Bring to Your Appointment

Gather these documents before your appointment to ensure a smooth, complete filing.

📋 Identity & Basic Info

  • Photo ID (driver's license or passport)
  • Social Security cards or ITIN letters for everyone on your return
  • Date of birth for all family members
  • Prior year tax return (if available)

💰 Income Documents

  • All W-2 forms from employers
  • 1099 forms (self-employment, interest, dividends, SSA)
  • Records of any other income received
  • Self-employment income & expense records

🏠 For Property Tax Refund (M1PR)

  • Certificate of Rent Paid (CRP) from your landlord — required for renters
  • Property Tax Statement from county — for homeowners
  • Total rent paid in the tax year

🏦 Banking & Other

  • Bank account and routing numbers for direct deposit
  • Health insurance coverage info (1095-A if marketplace)
  • Childcare provider name, address, and Tax ID
  • Student loan interest statements (1098-E)

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this really free? What's the catch?
Completely free. VITA (Volunteer Income Tax Assistance) is an IRS-sponsored program. Our volunteers are IRS-certified and there is no fee, no upsell, and no obligation.
What if my situation is complicated — multiple jobs, self-employment, or I'm a student?
Our volunteers handle W-2 income, simple self-employment (Schedule C), Social Security, unemployment, and most common situations. If your return requires a specialist (e.g., complex business returns or multiple state returns), we'll let you know at the appointment.
Do I need an appointment or can I walk in?
Appointments are strongly recommended and ensure the shortest wait. Walk-ins may be accommodated based on availability. Schedule online or by texting (651) 302-7258.
I don't have a Social Security Number — can I still get help?
Yes. We assist ITIN (Individual Taxpayer Identification Number) filers. Bring your ITIN letter and valid photo ID.
When is the deadline to file?
The federal tax deadline is typically April 15. The MN Property Tax Refund (M1PR) can be filed from late January through August 15. If you're owed a refund, there's no penalty for filing late — but file as soon as possible to receive your money sooner.
What languages are available?
We serve multilingual communities. Please contact us at (651) 302-7258 to ask about language support for your appointment.
