These SMS Terms of Service govern your participation in the Minnesota EquiVoice Partnership VITA text-messaging service operated through (651) 302-7258.

Minnesota EquiVoice Partnership is a Minnesota 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization that provides free Volunteer Income Tax Assistance (VITA) and Tax Counseling for the Elderly (TCE) services to eligible Minnesota residents.

1. Description of the SMS Service

The Minnesota EquiVoice Partnership VITA SMS service provides automated, conversational assistance concerning:

  • General federal and Minnesota tax questions;
  • Preliminary federal and Minnesota tax estimates;
  • Minnesota tax credits and refunds;
  • Minnesota Property Tax Refund and Renter's Credit information;
  • VITA eligibility and service information;
  • Tax-document checklists;
  • VITA locations and operating hours;
  • Appointment scheduling links; and
  • Follow-up information requested by the client.

The automated assistant may ask questions about your tax year, filing status, household size, income source, W-2 or 1099 income, approximate income, federal withholding, Minnesota withholding, rent paid, property taxes paid, and similar information needed to provide a preliminary estimate.

Messages are transactional and related to your tax inquiry or VITA service request. Minnesota EquiVoice Partnership does not use this program to send marketing, fundraising, advertising, or promotional messages.

2. How You Enroll

You may consent to receive SMS messages in either of the following ways:

Text-In Consent

You may initiate the service by voluntarily texting a tax-related question or request to (651) 302-7258. By texting this number, you consent to receive automated conversational responses from Minnesota EquiVoice Partnership concerning your inquiry.

Telephone Consent

During a call to (651) 302-7258, you may request an appointment or information link by text. Before sending the message, the automated telephone system will read an SMS disclosure and ask you to press 1 or say "yes" to consent. No text message will be sent unless you provide this affirmative consent.

Calling Minnesota EquiVoice Partnership does not, by itself, enroll you in SMS messaging. The complete telephone consent script is publicly documented at VITA Phone Line SMS Consent Disclosure.

3. Message Frequency

Message frequency varies according to your questions, responses, and requests.

A text-initiated tax-assistance conversation may involve multiple messages while the automated assistant asks follow-up questions and responds to your inquiry.

When you consent during a telephone call solely to receive an appointment link, you will ordinarily receive one requested message unless you reply with additional questions.

No unsolicited recurring or promotional messages are sent.

4. Message and Data Rates

Minnesota EquiVoice Partnership does not charge for its VITA SMS service.

Message and data rates may apply according to your mobile carrier and service plan. You are responsible for any charges imposed by your carrier.

5. Opting Out

You may stop receiving messages at any time by replying:

STOP

You may also use other commonly recognized opt-out keywords, including CANCEL, END, QUIT, UNSUBSCRIBE, or OPT OUT.

After opting out, you will receive one confirmation message. No additional messages will be sent unless you later initiate a new conversation or reply START to resubscribe.

Opting out of SMS messages does not prevent you from using Minnesota EquiVoice Partnership's telephone, website, in-person, or other VITA services.

7. Preliminary Estimates and Tax Disclaimer

Responses from the automated assistant are provided for general informational and educational purposes.

Any federal tax, Minnesota tax, credit, refund, Property Tax Refund, or Renter's Credit amount provided through SMS is a preliminary estimate based only on the information you voluntarily supply.

An estimate provided by the automated assistant:

  • Is not a completed or filed tax return;
  • Is not a guarantee of a refund, credit, liability, or eligibility determination;
  • May not account for every tax form, deduction, credit, limitation, or special circumstance;
  • Does not create a tax preparer-client, attorney-client, accountant-client, or fiduciary relationship; and
  • Should not be relied upon as a substitute for preparation and review of your complete tax return.

Actual tax results may differ materially after all income, deductions, credits, dependents, tax documents, and other relevant circumstances are reviewed. Please visit or schedule an appointment with a Minnesota EquiVoice Partnership VITA site to have your complete tax return prepared and reviewed.

8. Sensitive Information

SMS is not a secure channel for highly sensitive tax or financial information.

Do not send any of the following by text message:

  • Social Security numbers;
  • Individual Taxpayer Identification Numbers;
  • Bank account or routing numbers;
  • Complete dates of birth;
  • Passwords, PINs, or account credentials;
  • Photographs or copies of tax documents;
  • Identity documents; or
  • Payment-card information.

Minnesota EquiVoice Partnership may instruct you to use an approved secure process or attend a VITA site when sensitive information or documents are required.

9. Automated and Artificial-Intelligence Responses

Automated systems can misunderstand information, omit relevant considerations, or generate inaccurate results. You are responsible for reviewing the information provided and obtaining assistance from a qualified VITA volunteer when appropriate.

The web-based VITA assistant may also be available through Minnesota EquiVoice Partnership's website. Using the web assistant does not automatically enroll you in SMS messaging.
