Privacy Policy

Minnesota EquiVoice Partnership is committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how we use your information.

Effective Date: June 16, 2026  •  Last Updated: July 13, 2026

1. Who We Are

Minnesota EquiVoice Partnership ("MN-EVP," "we," "us," or "our") is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization based in Minnesota. We provide free VITA/TCE tax preparation services, financial education, healthcare outreach, civic engagement programs, and related community services to low-to-moderate income residents of Minnesota.

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect information when you interact with our website at mnequivoicepartnership.org, text or call us at (651) 302-7258, or use any of our services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information in the following ways:

Information you provide directly:

  • Name, phone number, and email address when scheduling an appointment
  • Tax-related information provided during a VITA/TCE tax preparation appointment (including income, household composition, and Social Security or ITIN numbers)
  • Questions and messages sent via text (SMS) or phone call to (651) 302-7258
  • Any information submitted through our website contact forms

Information collected automatically:

  • Your phone number when you call or text us (provided by your carrier)
  • Standard web server logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited) when you visit our website
  • Appointment scheduling data when you book through our online scheduling system (Microsoft Bookings)

3. Text (SMS) Messaging

Minnesota EquiVoice Partnership operates an automated VITA tax-assistance SMS service at (651) 302-7258.

How you may opt in:

  • Client-initiated text: You opt in by texting a tax-related question to (651) 302-7258. By texting us, you agree to receive automated conversational SMS responses concerning your tax inquiry, preliminary tax estimates, VITA appointments, locations, operating hours, document requirements, and related VITA services.
  • During a phone call: If you call (651) 302-7258 and wish to receive an appointment or information link by text, the automated voice system will ask your permission and read you the SMS disclosure. You must press 1 or say "yes" to agree. Calling our number does not automatically enroll you in text messaging.

SMS Service Disclosures

  • Message types: Automated responses to your tax questions, preliminary tax estimates, VITA appointment links, site information, operating hours, and document checklists. No marketing, fundraising, or promotional messages are sent.
  • Information collected: Depending on your questions, we may collect your tax year, filing status, household size, income source, approximate income, federal or Minnesota withholding, rent paid, property taxes paid, and other information relevant to a preliminary tax estimate.
  • Frequency: Message frequency varies according to your questions and responses. Messages are sent only in response to your inquiries or requests.
  • Cost: Our service is free. Message and data rates from your mobile carrier may apply.
  • Opt-out: Reply STOP at any time. You will receive one confirmation and no further messages.
  • Help: Reply HELP for assistance, or email info@mnequivoicepartnership.org or call (651) 302-7258.
  • Data sharing: We do not sell, rent, or share mobile telephone numbers, SMS opt-in data, or messaging consent information with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes.

Do not send Social Security numbers, ITINs, bank account or routing numbers, complete dates of birth, passwords, or copies or images of tax documents by text message.

No mobile information shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide free VITA/TCE tax preparation and respond to your tax questions
  • Schedule, confirm, and manage your appointments
  • Send you appointment reminders and relevant service information (if you have opted in)
  • Improve our programs and services
  • Comply with IRS VITA/TCE program requirements and reporting obligations
  • Operate and improve our website and digital services

We do not use your information for commercial marketing, and we do not sell your personal information to any third party.

5. Tax Return Information

Information collected during your VITA/TCE tax preparation appointment — including income, Social Security Numbers, and family information — is used solely to prepare your tax return.

IRS Confidentiality: All tax return information is handled in accordance with IRS Publication 4299 (Privacy, Confidentiality, and Civil Rights — A Public Trust). VITA/TCE volunteers are bound by IRS confidentiality requirements and may not disclose your tax information without your written consent, except as required by law.

Tax return documents are stored securely and retained only as long as required by IRS program guidelines. Electronic records are protected with appropriate access controls.

6. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. We may share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: Third-party platforms we use to operate our services, including Microsoft (appointment scheduling), Twilio (SMS and voice communications), and our website hosting provider. These providers are contractually required to protect your information and may not use it for their own purposes.
  • IRS and program partners: Aggregate, non-identifying program data may be reported to the IRS as required for VITA/TCE grant compliance. Individual tax information is never shared without your written consent.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or to protect the safety of individuals or the public.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. Our measures include:

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS) on our website
  • Secure, access-controlled systems for storing client data
  • Limiting access to personal information to staff and volunteers who need it to provide services
  • Annual IRS security awareness training for all VITA volunteers

No method of data transmission or storage is 100% secure. If you have concerns about the security of your information, please contact us.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal information, subject to legal retention requirements
  • Opt-out of SMS: Reply STOP to any text message from (651) 302-7258 at any time
  • Withdraw consent: Withdraw consent for any non-essential use of your information

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the information in Section 10 below.

9. Children's Privacy

Our website and text messaging services are not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately so we can delete it.
