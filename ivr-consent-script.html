VITA Phone Line — SMS Consent Disclosure

This page documents the automated voice script used to obtain caller consent before sending any text message from Minnesota EquiVoice Partnership's free VITA tax-assistance line at (651) 302-7258.

Effective Date: July 13, 2026  •  Phone: (651) 302-7258

1. Overview

When a caller dials (651) 302-7258, they reach Minnesota EquiVoice Partnership's free VITA automated phone service. The automated system never sends a text message without the caller's explicit, affirmative consent.

At any point during the call — whether during VITA service hours (Saturday–Sunday, 9 AM to 5 PM Central) or outside of those hours — callers may choose to receive an appointment scheduling link by text. Before any SMS is sent, the system reads the full consent disclosure in Section 4 and requires a keypress or verbal "yes."

2. Main IVR Script — During VITA Hours

Callers who reach the line during VITA service hours hear the following menu:

📞 Automated Voice System — Main Menu
System Thank you for calling Minnesota EquiVoice Partnership's free V-I-T-A tax preparation service. Our sites are open on Saturdays. The first and second Saturdays of each month we are at Saint Paul Public Library; the last two Saturdays at Rondo Community Library. Virtual appointments and document drop-off are also available. Press 1 to hear this message again, press 2 to leave a voicemail, press 3 to speak with a VITA volunteer, or press 4 to receive an appointment link by text.

If the caller presses 4, the system proceeds to the SMS Consent Disclosure in Section 4.

3. After-Hours and Holiday Script

Callers who reach the line outside of VITA service hours or on a federal holiday hear one of the following messages:

📞 After-Hours Message
System Thank you for calling Minnesota EquiVoice Partnership's free V-I-T-A tax preparation service. Our phone lines are open Saturdays from 9 AM to 5 PM. You may also visit mnequivoicepartnership.org to schedule online. Press 1 to leave a voicemail, or press 2 to receive an appointment link by text.
📞 Holiday Message
System Thank you for calling Minnesota EquiVoice Partnership. Our V-I-T-A sites are closed today in observance of the holiday. We are open Saturdays from 9 AM to 5 PM. Press 1 to leave a voicemail, or press 2 to receive an appointment link by text.

If the caller presses 2, the system proceeds to the SMS Consent Disclosure in Section 4.

4. SMS Consent Disclosure — Verbatim Script

Before any appointment link is sent by text, the automated system reads the following disclosure in full and waits for an affirmative response:

📞 Consent Disclosure (read verbatim by the system)
System "Would you like Minnesota EquiVoice Partnership VITA to text the appointment link to the number you are calling from? Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Press 1 or say yes to agree. Press 2 or say no to continue without a text."
Affirmative consent is required. The system accepts either a keypress of 1 or the spoken word "yes." No text message is sent unless one of these responses is received. If no response is received within 10 seconds, the caller is returned to the main menu and no message is sent.

5. What Happens After the Caller Responds

If the caller presses 1 or says "yes":

📞 Consent Accepted
System The appointment link has been sent to your phone.

One SMS is sent to the caller's number containing the VITA appointment scheduling link and STOP/HELP instructions (see Section 7).

If the caller presses 2, says "no," or does not respond within 10 seconds:

No SMS is sent. The caller is returned to the main menu.

6. Consent Audit Log

Every consent interaction is recorded automatically. Each record includes:

  • Twilio Call SID (unique call identifier)
  • Caller's phone number
  • Whether consent was given
  • Method of response (keypress or speech recognition)
  • The raw response received
  • Date and time of the consent decision (UTC)

Records are retained as evidence of consent in accordance with CTIA guidelines and carrier A2P 10DLC requirements.

7. SMS Message Sent After Consent

After affirmative consent, the caller receives exactly one text message:

📱 Text Message Sent to Caller
SMS Minnesota EquiVoice Partnership VITA: Here is the free VITA tax-preparation appointment information you requested: [appointment URL]. Saturdays at St. Paul Public Library (1st & 2nd Sat) or Rondo Community Library (last 2 Sat). Virtual & drop-off also available. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out.
